# History & Reports

Once you've logged a few things, Sprout Track helps you look back and spot
patterns. There are three places to do this: **Full Log**, **Calendar**, and
**Reports**. Reach any of them from the side menu or via Quick Search
(`Ctrl/Cmd + K`).

## Full Log

The **Full Log** is the complete, searchable history of everything logged for the
selected baby — feeds, sleeps, diapers, and all the rest, newest first.

Use it to:

- **Scroll back** through past days.
- **Filter** by activity type to focus on, say, only feeds or only diapers.
- **Tap an entry** to view, edit, or delete it.

It's the best place to answer "when was the last time…?" beyond what fits on the
home timeline.

## Calendar

The **Calendar** shows a **month view** of activity. It's handy for a
bird's-eye look — which days had lots of activity, spotting gaps, or jumping to a
specific date. Tap a day to see what was logged.

## Reports

**Reports** turn your logs into **charts and summaries**:

- **Growth charts** — weight, height/length, and head circumference plotted over
  time, with **percentile curves** for context. These are calculated from the
  baby's **birth date**, so they recalculate automatically if you update it (see
  [Editing Baby Details](editing-baby-details.md)).
- **Feeding, sleep, and diaper trends** — see daily/weekly patterns at a glance.
- **Pumping stats** — if you log pumping sessions.
- **Monthly summaries** — a roll-up of the month's activity.

> Growth percentiles need at least a couple of measurements to be useful — keep
> logging weight and height during checkups (see
> [Logging Activities → Measurement](logging-activities.md#measurement-including-temperature)).
