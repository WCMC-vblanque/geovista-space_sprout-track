# Editing Baby Details (including the Birth Date)

You can update a baby's information — **name, birth date, gender, and feed/diaper
warning thresholds** — at any time. This is especially handy when the birth date
is still an estimate (for example, before the baby is born) and needs to be
adjusted later.

## How to edit a baby

1. Open **Settings** — tap the gear icon, or use Quick Search (`Ctrl/Cmd + K`)
   and choose **Settings**.
2. Find the baby in the baby section of Settings.
3. Tap the **edit (pencil ✏️) icon** next to the baby's name.
4. Change whatever you need:
   - For the **birth date**, tap the date field and pick the new date from the
     calendar.
5. Tap **Update** to save.

## Good to know

- **The birth date can be changed as many times as you need** — there is no
  one-time limit. Set an estimated due date now and update it once the baby
  arrives.
- **Ages and growth charts update automatically.** The baby's age shown
  throughout the app, and the growth/percentile charts in Reports, are
  calculated from the birth date — they recalculate on their own when you change
  it.
- **Already-logged activities are not moved.** Each logged activity keeps its
  own real date and time; changing the birth date only affects age-based
  displays, not the activity history.
