# Logging Activities

Everything you track lives on the **Log Entry** home screen. Tap an activity
button, fill in the short form, and save — it shows up on the timeline right
away. Nothing is recorded until you tap save.

> **Reminder:** activities are logged for the **currently selected baby**. If you
> care for more than one, check the baby selector at the top first.

## The activity buttons

The row of round buttons is your toolbox. You can **drag to reorder** them so the
ones you use most sit first, and tap **Configure** to show or hide buttons.

The available activities are:

| Button | Use it for |
|--------|-----------|
| **Sleep** | Naps and night sleep (with a live timer). |
| **Feed** | Breast, bottle, or solids. |
| **Diaper** | Wet / dirty / mixed changes. |
| **Pump** | Pumping (expressing) sessions. |
| **Medicine** | Medications and doses. |
| **Measurement** | Weight, height, head circumference, **temperature**. |
| **Bath** | Bath time. |
| **Note** | Anything else worth remembering. |
| **Activity** | Play / tummy time (with a timer). |
| **Milestone** | Developmental milestones (first smile, rolling over…). |
| **Vaccine** | Vaccinations. |

## Sleep

1. Tap **Sleep** when the baby falls asleep — this **starts a live timer** and
   the button shows the sleep is in progress.
2. Tap **End Sleep** when they wake up to close the session.
3. You can also add a past sleep manually by adjusting the start/end times in the
   form.

The dashboard shows how long the current nap has lasted and the time since the
last sleep.

## Feed

Tap **Feed** and choose the type:

- **Breast** — start a feeding session with a **live timer**. You can **switch
  sides** (left/right), **pause**, and **resume**, then **End Feed** to save the
  total. Great for tracking which side was last used.
- **Bottle** — record the **amount** and unit (oz / ml) and whether it's breast
  milk or formula.
- **Solids** — record the food and amount.

> **Shortcut:** with Quick Search you can open the feed form already set to the
> right type — e.g. `Ctrl/Cmd + K` → type `breast` / `pecho`. See
> [Quick Search](quick-search.md).

## Diaper

Tap **Diaper** and pick **wet**, **dirty**, or **both**, then save. The dashboard
tracks the time since the last change — useful for the hydration check (wet
diapers).

## Pump

Tap **Pump** to log a pumping (expressing) session: amount per side and duration.
If breast-milk inventory tracking is enabled in Settings, pumped amounts feed
into your stored-milk balance.

## Measurement (including temperature)

Tap **Measurement** to record **weight, height/length, head circumference**, or
**temperature**. Weight and height flow into the growth charts in
[Reports](history-and-reports.md). Temperature is handy for tracking a fever.

## Medicine

Tap **Medicine** to record a medication, the dose, and the time. Keep a clear
history of what was given and when — especially useful when caregivers take
turns.

## Bath, Note, Activity, Milestone, Vaccine

- **Bath** — log bath time.
- **Note** — free-text for anything (mood, a rash, a doctor's comment…).
- **Activity** — play or tummy time, with an optional timer.
- **Milestone** — capture developmental milestones with the date.
- **Vaccine** — record vaccinations to keep an immunization history.

## Editing or deleting an entry

Tap any item on the **timeline** to open it. From there you can adjust the
details or the date/time, or delete it. Changes are saved immediately and the
timeline updates.
