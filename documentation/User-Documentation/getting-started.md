# Getting Started

This chapter walks you through signing in, finding your way around the home
screen, choosing which baby you're tracking, and installing the app on your
device.

## Signing in

Sprout Track has two ways to sign in, depending on how your family was set up.

### Login ID + Security PIN (shared family device)

Best for a tablet on the kitchen counter or a phone shared by caregivers.

1. Enter your **Login ID** — a short 2-character code for each caregiver.
2. Enter your **Security PIN** (at least 6 digits).
3. Tap **Go**.

> Each caregiver can have their own Login ID and PIN, so the app can show who
> logged each activity. If no individual logins are set up yet, the family uses a
> single shared PIN.

### Email + Password (personal account)

1. Enter your **email** and **password**.
2. Tap **Sign In**.
3. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot your password?** and follow the email link
   to reset it.

### First time? Set up your family

If this is a brand-new family, after signing in you'll be guided through a short
setup: name your family, add your first baby (name, birth date, gender), and
you're ready. You can change any of this later — see
[Editing Baby Details](editing-baby-details.md).

> The birth date can be an estimate (e.g. a due date before the baby is born) and
> updated later as many times as needed.

## A quick tour of the home screen

After signing in you land on **Log Entry** — your daily dashboard:

- **Top bar:** the **baby selector**, the **Quick Search** (🔍) button, your
  account/menu, and the side-menu trigger.
- **Activity buttons:** a row of round buttons (Sleep, Feed, Diaper, and more).
  Tap one to log that activity. You can **drag to reorder** them and tap
  **Configure** to choose which ones show.
- **Timeline:** below the buttons, a running list of everything logged today,
  newest first. Tap any entry to view or edit it.
- **Side menu:** open it (top-left) to reach Full Log, Calendar, Reports,
  Nursery Mode, Settings, language, and logout.

## Choosing which baby you're tracking

If your family has more than one baby, tap the **baby selector** in the top bar
and pick the right one. Everything you log, and everything the screens show,
applies to the **currently selected baby**. With a single baby, it's selected
automatically.

## Switching language

Sprout Track speaks several languages. Change it from **Settings**, the side
menu's language picker, or **Quick Search** (`Ctrl/Cmd + K` → type your language).
Your choice is remembered for next time.

## Light or dark theme

Toggle between light and dark appearance from **Settings** or via **Quick
Search** → *Toggle Light/Dark Theme*. Dark theme is great for night feeds.

## Install the app

Sprout Track is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — you can install it so it opens
fullscreen like a native app and keeps working with a weak or missing
connection.

- **On a phone/tablet:** open your browser menu and choose **Add to Home Screen**
  / **Install**.
- **On a computer (Chrome/Edge):** click the **Install** icon in the address bar,
  or the browser menu → **Install Sprout Track**.

Once installed, it works **offline** and syncs your entries when you're back
online.
