# Baby Tips

Sprout Track shows one development or care tip on the home dashboard, matched
to your baby's current age. Each tip comes from an official health authority
or midwife body — not a blog or generic parenting site — so you can trust
what you're reading and follow the link to read more.

## Where to find it

The tip card appears on the main **Log Entry** dashboard, below the quick-log
buttons, whenever a baby is selected. It updates automatically as your baby
gets older — no setup needed.

## Browsing other tips

Use the arrows on either side of the tip's title to look at tips from
earlier or later days. An arrow greys out once you reach the oldest or
newest tip currently available.

## Folding tips you've already read

The first time a tip is shown, it displays in full. After that, it
re-appears folded down to just its headline on later visits, so the
dashboard stays compact. Tap the headline (or the arrow next to it) to
expand a folded tip and read it again. This is remembered on your device,
so it may show expanded again if you open Sprout Track on a different phone
or browser.

## Choosing how often a new tip appears

Go to **Settings → Baby Tips → Tip Frequency** to choose:

- **Weekly** (default) — one tip per week of your baby's life.
- **Daily** — a new, shorter tip every day, with the weekly tip as that
  week's featured highlight.

## Sources

Tips draw from official sources including:

- **NHS** (UK)
- **Santé publique France**
- **AEPED**, the Spanish Association of Paediatrics
- **Sundhedsstyrelsen**, the Danish Health Authority

and other national health authorities across the tip bank. Each tip links
to the exact page it came from — tap **Source** under the tip to read the
original guidance in full.

## Coverage

Tips are currently available for your baby's first 12 weeks. Coverage for
later weeks/months is being added over time; until a given day has a tip,
the card simply doesn't appear on that day.

## A note on medical advice

These tips are general developmental and care information, not personalised
medical advice. If you have any concern about your baby's health or
development, contact your GP, paediatrician, midwife, or health visitor.
