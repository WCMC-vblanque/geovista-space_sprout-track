# Quick Search (Command Palette)

The Quick Search lets you jump straight to any section or action by typing a few
words — no need to open the side menu and hunt for what you want. It works like
the search box in apps such as VS Code, Notion, or Slack.

## How to open it

You can open Quick Search in two ways:

- **Keyboard:** press **`Ctrl + K`** (Windows/Linux) or **`Cmd + K`** (Mac).
- **Search button:** tap the **magnifying glass (🔍)** icon in the top bar,
  next to the baby selector. This is the easiest way on a phone or tablet.

## How to use it

1. Open Quick Search.
2. Start typing a few letters of what you want — for example `cal`, `rep`,
   `diaper`, or `dark`.
3. The list filters as you type. Use the **arrow keys (↑ ↓)** to move the
   highlight, or just move your mouse / tap with your finger.
4. Press **`Enter`** (or click/tap the item) to go there.
5. Press **`Esc`** to close without doing anything.

The search ignores accents and capitalization (typing `panal` still finds
"pañal"), and it understands words in different languages, so it works whether
your app is set to English, Spanish, French, or another language.

## What you can do from Quick Search

**Quick Log** — record an activity in a couple of keystrokes. The matching form
opens already set to the right type, so you just confirm the time/amount and
save (nothing is recorded until you save):

- **Log feeding — Breast** (try typing `ali pecho`, `breast`, or `teta`)
- **Log feeding — Bottle** (`bottle`, `biberón`)
- **Log feeding — Solids** (`solids`, `sólidos`)
- **Log diaper** (`diaper`, `pañal`)
- **Log sleep** (`sleep`, `dormir`)
- **Log pumping** (`pump`, `extracción`, `sacaleche`)
- **Log medicine** (`medicine`, `medicina`, `medicamento`)
- **Log measurement** (`measurement`, `medición`, `peso`, `talla`)
- **Log bath** (`bath`, `baño`)
- **Log note** (`note`, `nota`)
- **Log activity** (`activity`, `actividad`, `juego`)
- **Log milestone** (`milestone`, `hito`)
- **Log vaccine** (`vaccine`, `vacuna`)

The list is ordered with the most-used actions first. These open on the
currently selected baby. If you track more than one baby, select the right baby
first.

**Navigation**

- **Log Entry** — the daily dashboard where you record activities
- **Full Log** — the searchable history of everything logged
- **Calendar** — month view of activity history
- **Reports** — growth charts and monthly reports
- **Nursery Mode** — fullscreen, large-button mode

**Actions**

- **Settings** — open the settings panel
- **Baby Quick Info** — open the quick info card for the selected baby
- **Toggle Light/Dark Theme** — switch between light and dark mode
- **Logout** — sign out

**Language**

- Switch the app language instantly to any of the supported languages
  (English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Nederlands, Português, Română).

## Tip

Quick Search is the fastest way to move around the app once you know it. Try
opening it with `Ctrl/Cmd + K` and typing the first few letters of where you
want to go.
