# Settings & People

Open **Settings** from the gear icon, the side menu, or Quick Search
(`Ctrl/Cmd + K` → *Settings*). This is where you manage babies, the people who
help care for them, and how the app behaves.

## Babies

- **Add a baby** — create a new baby with name, birth date, and gender.
- **Edit a baby** — tap the **pencil ✏️** next to a baby to update their name,
  **birth date**, gender, or feed/diaper **warning thresholds**. Full details in
  [Editing Baby Details](editing-baby-details.md).
- **Warning thresholds** — set how many hours since the last feed or diaper before
  the dashboard flags it, so the timers match your baby's routine.

## Caretakers (the people who log)

Sprout Track supports multiple caregivers so you can see **who** logged what.

- **Add a caretaker** — give each person a short **Login ID** (2 characters) and a
  **Security PIN**.
- **Change PIN** — update a caretaker's PIN at any time.
- **System PIN mode** — if no individual caretakers are set up, the family shares
  a single security PIN. Once you add real caretakers, individual logins take
  over.

> See [Getting Started → Signing in](getting-started.md#signing-in) for how these
> logins are used.

## Preferences

- **Language** — switch the app's language; your choice is remembered.
- **Theme** — light or dark appearance.
- **Default units** — choose your defaults for bottle amounts and solids
  (oz / ml, etc.).
- **Date & time format** — pick **12-hour** or **24-hour** time and your date
  format.
- **Dashboard status** — tune what the home screen highlights.

## Breast Milk Tracking (optional)

Turn on **Breast Milk Inventory Tracking** to keep a running balance of stored
(pumped) milk. When enabled, pumping sessions add to your inventory and bottle
feeds with breast milk draw it down.

## Notifications

- **Enable Notifications** to get push reminders/alerts on your device.
- You may be asked to allow notifications in your browser the first time.

> Proactive **health alerts** (e.g. "no feed logged in 4+ hours", fever) are
> planned — see the [roadmap](../../TODO.md).

## Admin & advanced (for the family organizer)

The **Admin** area holds advanced, family-wide settings. Most caregivers won't
need these:

- **Authentication settings** — session/idle timeouts and login rules.
- **API Keys** — create keys for external integrations and **webhooks** (e.g.
  home-automation). Copy a key right after creating it — it's shown only once.
- **Family management** — system-wide options for the family organizer or
  administrator.

> **Security note:** never share your PINs, passwords, or API keys. Your family's
> data stays private to your family.
