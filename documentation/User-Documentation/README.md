# Sprout Track — User Manual

Welcome! Sprout Track helps you and everyone who cares for your little one keep
track of sleep, feeding, diapers, growth, and all the little moments in between.

This manual is written for **caregivers, not engineers** — plain language,
step by step. Hand it to grandparents, babysitters, or daycare so they can use
the app with confidence.

---

## 🚀 Quick Onboarding (5 minutes)

New here? Do just these steps and you're ready to track:

1. **Open the app and sign in.**
   - If you were given a **Login ID + Security PIN**, enter those. (Login ID is
     2 characters; the PIN is 6+ digits.)
   - If you created an **account with email**, sign in with your email and
     password.
   - First time setting up a family? Follow the on-screen setup wizard to create
     your family and add your first baby.

2. **Pick the baby.** If more than one baby is set up, tap the **baby selector**
   at the top and choose who you're tracking. (Just one baby? It's already
   selected.)

3. **Log your first activity.** On the home screen (**Log Entry**) you'll see a
   row of round buttons — Sleep, Feed, Diaper, and more. Tap one, fill in the
   quick form, and tap save. That's it — it appears instantly on the timeline
   below.

4. **Learn the one shortcut that saves the most time:** press **`Ctrl + K`**
   (or **`Cmd + K`** on Mac), type a couple of words (e.g. `feed`, `diaper`,
   `pañal`), and jump straight to any screen or log form. See
   [Quick Search](quick-search.md).

5. **(Optional) Install it like an app.** In your browser's menu choose
   *Install* / *Add to Home Screen* so it opens fullscreen and works even with a
   weak connection. See [Getting Started](getting-started.md#install-the-app).

> **Tip for night feeds:** turn on **Nursery Mode** for a dark, fullscreen
> screen with big buttons you can tap half-asleep. See
> [Nursery Mode](nursery-mode.md).

### A few things that apply everywhere

- **Everything is private to your family.** You only ever see your own family's
  babies and history.
- **Nothing is saved until you tap save.** Opening a log form never records
  anything on its own.
- **Your language is remembered.** Switch it anytime from Settings or Quick
  Search; the app keeps your choice.
- **Works offline.** As an installed app it keeps working with a poor or missing
  connection and syncs when you're back online.
